# SignalForge Founder-First Product UI Update

## Product problem

The previous Founder page exposed internal routing, Brain labels, C01-C14 terminology, machine queues, and system semantics before answering the Founder questions that matter. A technically correct surface was still operationally confusing.

The update changes the interaction hierarchy without changing market truth, evidence authority, Radar gates, Brain authority, or execution scheduling.

## Founder-first hierarchy

The default homepage now answers, in order:

1. Is there anything worth acting on today?
2. What is the single highest-value Founder task, if any?
3. What opportunity directions are still worth keeping alive?
4. What is AI handling without Founder attention?
5. What is the current truth state (including UNVALIDATED calibration)?

Raw operating lanes, Candidate evidence queues, C01-C14 details, and routing internals remain available but are collapsed under an advanced disclosure.

## Primary Founder action

The page derives "待你處理" from the actual suggested Founder/Market action queue, not from the execution governor's machine-route counts. This prevents a Founder Discovery recommendation from disappearing just because its corresponding Radar case is not represented in the same routing projection.

Known actions and blockers are translated to plain Founder language. Example:

- IDENTIFY_REACHABLE_BUYER_CHANNEL → 找出你真的接觸得到的買家管道
- BUYER_ACCESS_NOT_ESTABLISHED → 我們還不知道去哪裡找到真正會買的人

The action card exposes what to do, success signal, failure signal, sample target, and a registration button while preserving the existing pre-test / market-action truth boundary.

## Strategic thesis surface

Default thesis cards now show:

- whether the direction is actually actionable or still premature;
- distance from Eason's demonstrated capability;
- trust burden;
- biggest blocking unknown;
- current task capability state;
- next decision-changing action.

Raw strategic track labels, structural dimensions, IDs, and member Candidate objects remain available through drill-down instead of dominating the default view.

## Other navigation surfaces

- Sidebar labels are Founder-language rather than system taxonomy.
- Thesis Detail is rewritten around "what should I do" and "why can't I do it yet", with technical truth under Advanced.
- Research is labeled as AI research progress and explicitly says most of it requires no Founder attention.
- Search defaults to "商機與問題" vs "原始資料" instead of intelligence/corpus jargon.

## Truth boundary

This is a presentation and workflow-clarity update only.

It does not alter:

- C01-C14 claim state;
- evidence validation thresholds;
- market-action outcome rules;
- Brain strategic authority;
- production admission;
- execution governor thresholds;
- Market Calibration.

Market Calibration remains dependent on real qualified outcomes and is not changed by this UI update.
