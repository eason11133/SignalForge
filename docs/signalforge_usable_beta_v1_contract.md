# SignalForge Usable Beta v1 Contract

## Product line
Founder can move from Opportunity Card to:
1. published evidence detail;
2. explicit Known / Unknown / Contradicted truth snapshot;
3. bounded SHADOW Research More;
4. full published refresh trigger;
5. ChatGPT discussion handoff;
6. source traceability.

## Truth boundary
- U14/U15 Research More is SHADOW.
- Retrieved candidates never mutate C01-C14 directly.
- WTP remains UNKNOWN unless formal published adjudication supports it.
- Search failure is not negative evidence.
- U23-U28 discovery intelligence remains SHADOW and does not gate Founder use.

## Beta acceptance
- Product UI is usable without reading telemetry.
- No automatic product ideation.
- No false market-validation percentage.
- Discussion handoff is zero additional SignalForge LLM call.
