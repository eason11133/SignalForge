# SignalForge — Solo-Founder Transition Opportunity Contract v1

## Product boundary
SignalForge does **discovery + filtering + evidence**. It does not invent a product to rescue a weak problem.

## Founder opportunity definition
A Founder-priority opportunity must be a **captureable transition gap**, not merely an important problem:

1. **Problem atom** — a specific actor is performing a concrete workflow and a specific step repeatedly fails or remains manual.
2. **Change signal** — technology, platform, regulation, cost curve, or behavior has changed. Missing evidence stays UNKNOWN.
3. **Adoption gap** — the buyer/workflow has not fully adapted to that change; the old/current workaround is observed.
4. **Economic necessity** — the current state creates observed time, labor, revenue, cost, delay, or existing-spend consequences owned by a real buyer.
5. **Solo captureability** — a single founder has a realistic path to build, test, sell, and operate the first version using existing technology; frontier research, specialized hardware, large capital, or unproven specialist expertise are blockers.

`Market importance × zero captureability = not a Founder opportunity.`

## Classification
- `RESEARCH_THEME` — broad, frontier, or phenomenon-level problem. Keep for research context; do not place in Founder priority.
- `SOLO_WATCH` — sufficiently atomic, but buyer/transition/economic/capture evidence remains incomplete.
- `SOLO_INVESTIGATE` — transition gap, economic necessity, and solo capture path are supported enough for focused Founder research.
- `SOLO_VALIDATE` — the same, plus the existing reality gates are strong enough for a real customer validation experiment.

## Hard truth rules
- UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN.
- Attention / number of signals cannot override a blocker.
- A repository/source entity is not automatically a current solution.
- Generic AI/API integration does not prove model research, GPU kernel, or distributed-inference capability.
- No product wedge, MVP, or monetization story is generated to make a weak opportunity look viable.
- Empty Founder priority is valid. SignalForge must not manufacture filler opportunities.

## Founder acceptance
A real Top opportunity must let the Founder answer from evidence:
- Who exactly has the problem?
- What exact workflow is failing or still manual?
- What changed?
- What has not caught up?
- What observable economic cost exists today?
- Why is the current workaround still used?
- Why can one founder plausibly capture the first version?

If these cannot be answered, it is not yet a Founder-priority opportunity.
