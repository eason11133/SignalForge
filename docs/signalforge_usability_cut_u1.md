# SignalForge Usability Cut U1

## Product boundary
SignalForge is Opportunity Intelligence. U1 does not generate products. It exposes a bounded Founder research shortlist so the Founder can discuss evidence-backed problem hypotheses with ChatGPT.

## Mature research adopted
- Need/solution-pair search: open-web evidence can surface early-stage needs and user-developed solutions; preserve evidence fragments rather than requiring a complete problem statement in one document.
- Structural/semantic relationship review: lexical similarity is retrieval only; same-problem corroboration requires relationship checking.
- Weak supervision: incomplete evidence can abstain or enter research instead of being hard-rejected.
- Truth discovery/provenance: duplicate or dependent content does not count as recurrence.
- Human/AI judgment boundary: constrained LLM review may reject mismatched relationships or obvious research noise, but cannot create facts, evidence, products, market scores, or market validation.

## U1 usability rule
An evidence-backed research hypothesis requires:
1. stable evidence-derived problem-family identity;
2. at least two independent firsthand problem evidence items;
3. sufficient research priority;
4. semantic relationship review when available.

Facets are optional metadata and never a prerequisite for the hypothesis.

A single-source lead may appear only as `RESEARCH_LEAD_NEEDS_CORROBORATION` and cannot be promoted by the semantic auditor.
