# SignalForge Founder-Usable Cut U5

U5 turns the U4 discussion surface into a stricter Founder-facing research product.

Key adoption changes:
- dependence-aware truth discovery: copied/cross-posted evidence cannot manufacture recurrence;
- source-role priors: jobs/employer records, supplier pitches, policy context and positive capability stay out of user-demand recurrence;
- user-innovation Need–Solution signals require firsthand UGC + explicit self-build + own-need language;
- Founder discussion-readiness filters contextless/narrow technical noise without deleting it from the research layer;
- external-enabler / supply context needs strong entity or structural relation; generic workflow overlap cannot create a why-now claim;
- recovery has a fresh bounded per-run budget, diversified community/supply sources, and parses only new recovery docs;
- User-Innovation signals can trigger recurrence search but never count as independent demand by themselves.

Run:

```powershell
python run_signalforge_founder_usable_u5.py
```

A `READY_FOR_FOUNDER_DISCUSSION` result means only that at least one research card is worth Founder attention and is evidence-role safe. It does **not** mean market validation, WTP, product wedge, or venture attractiveness has been proven.
