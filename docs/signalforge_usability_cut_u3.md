# SignalForge Usability Cut U3 — Full Quality Sweep

U3 is not a micro-patch for U2. It updates the whole evidence-to-Founder research path that can improve without inventing frontier market truth.

## Mature-method adoption

- Forced cached-observation schema migration: stale rows are re-annotated even when an older `evidence_disposition` exists.
- Multi-label feedback roles: problem report, feature request, workaround/usage, positive capability, supplier pitch, user-innovation Need–Solution signal, policy context.
- Evidence-derived partial Need Frames: actor, workflow, actions, failure modes, objects, consequence, workaround and specific target; missing fields remain unknown.
- Balanced retrieve→rerank corroboration with structural features and a specific-target conflict veto to prevent superficially similar technical issues from merging.
- Raw market-supply documents materialize as Market State; supply existence never implies payment or willingness to pay.
- User-innovation Need–Solution signals are kept separately and may contextualize needs, but never count as independent customer recurrence.
- Recovery uses sanitized Need-Frame query terms, targetability/value/diversity selection, same-product app reviews, StackExchange where appropriate, and bounded public Reddit search fallback.
- Founder shortlist shows only role-correct hypotheses, user innovations and direct need leads, with Need Frames and explicit unknowns.

## Frontier boundary

U3 does not claim to solve external-enabler × workflow-lag opportunity linkage, market attractiveness, willingness-to-pay, founder captureability or market validation. Those remain separate research/test questions.

## Runtime

`python run_signalforge_usability_cut_u3.py`

The final acceptance is human review of the live H/U/L cards, not an internal PASS label.
