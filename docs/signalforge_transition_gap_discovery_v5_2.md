# SignalForge V5.2 — Change-First Transition-Gap Discovery

Founder target: **solo-founder captureable transition gaps**, not large world problems.

Discovery now has two routes:

1. **Change-first:** find an already-real technology/platform/regulation/cost/behavior change, then connect it to a specific lagging workflow with concrete operational cost.
2. **Pain-first:** retain the existing narrow Actor × workflow × failure path, but broad/frontier/phenomenon problems stay Research Themes.

A change-first candidate is allowed only when two persisted source documents are semantically verified as one coherent market/workflow. The engine does not generate an app, SaaS, AI layer, coach, MVP, market size, or willingness-to-pay claim to rescue a weak pair.

The first semantic gate requires: explicit change, specific actor/workflow, explicit legacy/adoption lag, observed cost, same market, and a solo-scale problem. Frontier model/GPU/kernel/hardware problems are rejected.

A verified source pair can support the transition dimension before C07/C12 are separately researched, but it does **not** bypass buyer reality (C05), material consequence (C03), or company/solo captureability (C09). Market outcome and predictive accuracy remain UNVALIDATED until real external outcomes exist.
