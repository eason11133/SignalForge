# SignalForge Evidence-Gap VOI U13

U13 preserves the U9-U12 Founder semantic gate and adds persistent card-linked evidence acquisition.

- Every recovery request carries a Founder/research anchor id, evidence-gap type, surface, and query provenance.
- Downstream observations are attributed back to the card/gap that caused the search.
- Attribution means retrieved-for-this-gap, not that the gap is semantically closed.
- A conservative Value-of-Information proxy prioritizes bounded searches by decision impact, empirical evidence yield, diminishing returns, and prior failed attempts.
- Founder cards display evidence-gap progress and recommend switching query/surface after repeated low-yield attempts.
- Supply evidence can update CURRENT_SOLUTION_SUPPLY only; it never closes independent need recurrence or WTP.
