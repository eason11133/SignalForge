# SignalForge Brain v2 — Full-System Longitudinal Intelligence Architecture

## Mission

Long-term accumulate market problems, use short-term evidence to update them, detect structural transitions, and identify solo-founder-capturable Zip2-class opportunities.

This release deliberately separates atomic evidence truth from structural opportunity intelligence.

## Authority planes

### Production Radar — atomic truth owner

Radar's validated Evidence Ledger remains the sole owner of C01–C14 claim state. Brain v2 cannot write or relax those claims. Unvalidated CandidateEvidence has no structural truth authority.

### Brain v2 — derived longitudinal structure owner

Brain v2 owns only derived objects:

1. ProblemAtom
2. TransitionAtom
3. ProblemLineage
4. TransitionHypothesis (staging; no commercial authority)
5. TransitionLineage
6. ExistingSystem
7. StructuralBridgeHypothesis (research-only; no intersection authority)
8. StructuralIntersection
9. OpportunityThesis
10. ResearchQuestion

Derived objects are event-sourced and replayable. UNKNOWN is preserved rather than completed heuristically.

### Shadow U23–U28 / FrameGraph

FrameGraph remains discovery-only Shadow intelligence with ZERO Production authority. A graph edge, analogy, novelty signal, or latent relation is never buyer/WTP/opportunity truth.

## Why this is not a Candidate backlog

A Candidate is an early observation/hypothesis container. It is not a persistent problem identity and is not an opportunity. Candidate ID churn must not destroy a ProblemLineage. Multiple candidates can compress into one lineage. A thesis exists only when a problem lineage and structural evidence create an eligible intersection.

## 12-dimensional structural vector

- Problem Persistence
- Transition Strength
- System Mismatch
- Economic Materiality
- Workaround Intensity
- Buyer Formation
- Gap Durability
- Asset Accessibility
- Distribution Leverage
- Incumbent Response Power
- Captureability
- Expansion Surface

The vector is not averaged into one authoritative score. Fatal UNKNOWN/REFUTED dimensions remain visible.

## Pair-scoped structural truth

Structural evidence is scoped to the exact ProblemLineage × TransitionLineage intersection. Global strength in a TransitionLineage cannot prove that the transition causes/exposes every problem it touches. A fully `SUPPORTED` system mismatch requires at least two independent direct pair units from two independent source families; one direct pair link remains `PARTIAL`.

Commercial/founder claims are also scoped to the exact thesis candidate slice. C05–C14 support on another member of a broad ProblemLineage cannot leak into a structural thesis. On multi-candidate scopes, one supported candidate yields at most `PARTIAL`; two scoped candidates are required before derived coverage may say `SUPPORTED`. Radar remains the atomic truth owner throughout.

Explicit transition metadata such as technology, regulation, platform, or transition mechanism is a high-authority identity anchor. Conflicting explicit subjects block a TransitionLineage merge even if generic text is otherwise identical. When no explicit subject exists, the field stays UNKNOWN rather than being invented.

## Zip2-class rule

Zip2-class classification requires a structural mismatch, not merely a complaint, trend, recency spike, or high attention score. `ZIP2_HIGH_CONVICTION` cannot self-certify without the required evidence dimensions. Expansion remains UNKNOWN unless explicit verified evidence supports it.

## Meaningful Change

Freshness alone is not a meaningful change. Structural change is emitted only when a lineage/system/intersection/thesis semantic state changes. Research-attempt telemetry and timestamps cannot masquerade as market change.

## Research VOI

Brain v2 produces decision-critical research advisory based on uncertainty and potential decision impact. Production gate groups retain first priority. Brain can reorder only source groups already supported by Production. It cannot invent a scraper/adapter, weaken a gate, or write Radar truth.

## Runtime isolation

Production SignalForge commits its PASS state first. Only afterward does it launch Brain v2 as a separate derived process. Brain failure can make Brain stale/fail but cannot convert a successful Production truth cycle into FAIL.

## G2 structural admission closure (real-corpus finding)

The R2 live acceptance exposed a semantic scaling defect rather than merely a timing defect:
1,390 of 1,434 transition observations were admitted as persistent transition atoms, producing
599 TransitionLineages and 1,273 StructuralIntersections, while zero transition intersections
were thesis-eligible. The root cause was treating validated timing/C12/why-now context as
persistent transition identity.

G2 changes the object admission boundary:

- timing/context observation != persistent TransitionLineage;
- C12 SUPPORT is opportunity-window evidence, not a transition identity by itself;
- persistent transition admission requires either a verified direct problem-transition pair or
  an explicit structural subject on a strong transition relation;
- generic why-now/ecosystem/C12 rows remain upstream context and are not duplicated into the
  Brain event ledger;
- StructuralIntersection is materialized only when pair evidence can affect a thesis: direct
  pair evidence, or a narrowly corroborated explicit-subject mismatch across multiple candidates;
- shared-candidate coincidence is coverage, not an intersection object.

The Brain therefore persists decision-bearing structural memory rather than a second copy of all
market/timing evidence. Radar remains the sole owner of C01-C14 atomic truth.

## G3 — Structural Recall without authority leakage

G2 deliberately fixed transition over-admission. The live result proved the precision boundary was
working, but also exposed the next failure mode: a precision-only Brain can end with persistent
ProblemLineages and supported transition context while materializing no StructuralIntersection.
G3 adds two staging objects between observation context and decision-bearing structure. Neither is
commercial truth.

### TransitionHypothesis

`TransitionHypothesis` is a bounded corroboration layer for verified context that does not yet have
single-row persistent-transition authority.

A context cluster may become `PROMOTABLE` only when:

- every member is verified and has a known transition driver;
- at least two independent source families and two independent content units converge;
- semantic identity is complete-link coherent rather than chain-merged;
- the observations cross candidates or persist for at least 14 days; and
- identity safety is satisfied.

Identity safety is intentionally asymmetric:

- inside one already-validated ProblemLineage, independent context may corroborate a problem-scoped
  transition;
- across different ProblemLineages, promotion additionally requires a genuinely sparse shared
  identity anchor. Broad vocabulary such as adoption, compliance, process, workflow, market or
  timing cannot create a global transition merely because it is uncommon in one driver cohort.

Promotion grants only persistent `TransitionLineage` identity. It cannot support C01-C14, buyer,
WTP, gap, captureability or opportunity truth.

### StructuralBridgeHypothesis

`StructuralBridgeHypothesis` exists when a persistent problem and a persistent transition have a
specific enough identity overlap to justify targeted research, but no validated pair evidence yet
exists. Its authority is exactly:

`RESEARCH_TARGET_ONLY_NOT_STRUCTURAL_INTERSECTION`

The object may create a pre-thesis ResearchQuestion. It may never create a StructuralIntersection or
OpportunityThesis by semantic similarity alone. The evidence acquisition target is direct proof that
the transition changes/exposes this problem or makes the inherited system mismatch economically
important.

The G3 flow is therefore:

```text
Verified observations
   |                         |
ProblemAtom             Transition context
   |                         |
ProblemLineage       TransitionHypothesis (staging)
   |                         |
   |                  TransitionLineage
   |                         |
   +-------- StructuralBridgeHypothesis --------> Research VOI
   |                 (research-only)
   |
   +-------- validated pair evidence -----------+
                                                 |
                                     StructuralIntersection
                                                 |
                                        OpportunityThesis
                                                 |
                                   Zip2 Gate + 12-D Vector
```

## Thesis evolution, death and revival

OpportunityThesis identity is longitudinal. Recomputing the same semantic thesis does not increment
its revision. A revision is created only when semantic thesis state changes. Lifecycle history records:

- `CREATED`
- `STATE_CHANGED`
- `DIED`
- `REVIVED`

Death states remain explicit falsification outcomes such as solved/no durable gap, no buyer,
stalled transition, non-captureable structure or incumbent absorption. A later evidence change can
revive the same thesis identity; the Brain does not erase the prior death history.

## Zip2 Gate + Vector

G3 makes the Zip2 adjudication contract explicit as `GATE_PLUS_VECTOR_NOT_AVERAGE_SCORE`.
UNKNOWN or PARTIAL pillars cannot be averaged away by strong scores elsewhere.

Mandatory structural/economic/capture pillars include persistent problem, structural transition,
system mismatch, economic necessity, buyer formation, durable gap and new-entrant captureability.
Distribution leverage, asset accessibility and expansion surface remain visible decision dimensions.
A thesis cannot self-certify Zip2-high status while a mandatory pillar is unresolved.

## Derivation-engine migration

Brain derived state is versioned independently of Production truth. A canonical Production truth
fingerprint being unchanged is not sufficient for a no-op after Brain code/schema changes.

G3 no-op requires all three to match:

1. Production truth fingerprint;
2. Brain derivation engine version;
3. Brain schema version.

A G2 -> G3 upgrade therefore forces one full derived rebuild even when Production truth is byte-for-byte
unchanged. The rebuild is recorded as `global:derivation_engine_upgrade`; afterward the identical
truth returns to the fast no-op path. This prevents stale G2 derived objects from surviving forever
behind a valid Production truth fingerprint.
