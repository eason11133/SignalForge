# SignalForge Part 4 — Opportunity Decision System

Part 4 turns existing SignalForge truth into a Founder decision surface. It does **not** create a second market-truth engine.

## Founder-facing track projection

Canonical Brain strategy remains unchanged:

- `FAST_VALIDATION` → Founder projection `CASH`
- `BOTH` → `BOTH`
- `ZIP2_STRUCTURAL` → `ZIP2`
- `NEITHER` → `PARK`

The projection never rewrites `strategic_track`, C01–C14, Published evidence, or Market Calibration.

## Product capabilities

1. **Founder Fit** — replays existing `founder_addressability` dimensions such as buyer access, legitimacy, domain knowledge and right-to-win.
2. **Structural Mapper** — exposes existing Brain structural dimensions and ZIP2 readiness without inventing structural facts.
3. **Wedge Ladder** — shows Problem → Buyer → Unresolved Gap → Existing Spend → Reachable Buyer → Testable Wedge → Real Paid Outcome. The final stage can only be supported by a real market outcome.
4. **Compare / Battle** — transparent lexicographic ranking; no opaque weighted total score.
5. **Ranking-change explanation** — states what new evidence would materially change the current ordering.
6. **Do Not Research** — suppresses repeated same-type research when it cannot change the decision.
7. **Promotion / Demotion + Trigger Watch** — states which world-state changes justify reopening or changing a track. These are review conditions, not automatic monitoring results or truth mutations.
8. **Founder Resource Allocation** — advisory weekly time blocks based on cash need, long-term importance and current decision readiness. It never auto-schedules, spends, or executes.
9. **Ask SignalForge** — bounded deterministic queries over the current decision projection: best/compare, cash/first-dollar, ZIP2/long-term, PARK, Trigger Watch, Do Not Research.

## Authority boundary

- Market Truth writes: `0`
- New C dimensions: `0`
- Founder-facing track mutation: `0`
- Resource allocation authority: advisory only
- Promotion / demotion authority: review trigger only
- Market Calibration: unchanged until real outcomes exist

## Acceptance philosophy

Part 4 acceptance tests a real decision-shaped fixture portfolio with four different cases (`CASH`, `BOTH`, `ZIP2`, `PARK`) and verifies that the Founder can rank, compare, see what changes the ranking, inspect structural/Founder-fit basis, identify trigger conditions and allocate bounded weekly time without creating market truth.

External/live market validation is not claimed by this engineering acceptance.
