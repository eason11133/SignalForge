# SignalForge Usability Cut U2

## Purpose
Turn the U1 Founder shortlist into a research-grounded evidence surface without conflating customer need, solution context, supplier promotion, policy opinion, or user innovation.

## Adopted mature-method behavior
- Multi-label feedback taxonomy: problem report, feature request, workaround/usage, positive capability/feature strength, supplier pitch, policy context, user-innovation Need-Solution signal.
- Partial/implicit requirements remain valid evidence; a primary signature is optional.
- User innovations are retained as early Need-Solution signals but never count as independent customer recurrence.
- Supplier/solution statements and positive capability descriptions are context, not customer pain.
- Founder hypotheses require independent need-role evidence and a bounded same-problem relationship audit.
- Single-source Founder leads are bounded, role-filtered and fail closed on malformed semantic audit output.

## Truth boundary
U2 does not claim that a Need-Solution signal is market demand, that a research hypothesis is a venture opportunity, or that any item has willingness-to-pay or market validation. Those remain downstream evidence/test questions.
